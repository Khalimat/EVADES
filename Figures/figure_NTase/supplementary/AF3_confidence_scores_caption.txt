Supplementary Figure. AlphaFold 3 confidence for NTase-NTP(pair)-Mg2+ co-folding, with up to five independent AlphaFold 3 seeds (five diffusion samples each, up to 25 models per condition) per NTase per NTP pair. Rows: (top) ipTM, (bottom) pTM. Columns: Bacillus phage Bcp1 NTase (YP_009031408) and its homolog from Erinnyis ello granulovirus (YP_009091875). Each NTase was co-folded with one of 10 unordered NTP pairs drawn from {ATP, GTP, CTP, UTP} and three Mg2+ ions. Points: every model available so far; box: median and interquartile range; black diamond with error bar: per-seed mean +/- SD (n = seeds available). Annotated omnibus p-value: permutation test for any difference among the NTP-pair groups with data so far (per-seed means, the seed as the independent replicate). All pairwise NTP-pair comparisons (two-sided exact permutation test on the per-seed means, Benjamini-Hochberg FDR-corrected within each family of 45 comparisons per protein per score) are reported in AF3_confidence_scores_pairwise_stats.tsv (machine-readable) and AF3_confidence_scores_pairwise_stats.md (formatted, sorted by q).

IPTM phage-vs-viral Pearson r=0.835 (bootstrap 95% CI [0.616, 0.967], n=10/10 NTP pairs with data for both proteins so far)
PTM phage-vs-viral Pearson r=0.807 (bootstrap 95% CI [0.604, 0.966], n=10/10 NTP pairs with data for both proteins so far)
