Supplementary Figure 6. AlphaFold 3 confidence for the predicted structures shown in the main figures. Each complex is shown as a cartoon, with ligands as sticks, coloured by per-atom pLDDT (dark blue, very high, > 90; light blue, high, 70-90; yellow, low, 50-70; orange, very low, < 50), next to its predicted aligned error (PAE) matrix (0-31.75 Å; darker green, lower expected error); black lines mark chain boundaries. The heading of each panel gives the AlphaFold 3 ranking score, ipTM and pTM. Complexes: AcrIC5-PaCas8c, AcrIC3-PaCas3, AcrIB4-LsCas8b1 and AcrIIA26-SpyCas9 (chain A, Cas protein; chain B, Acr protein); Ocr-DISARM DrmMII and Ocr-Panchino gp28 (chain A, Ocr; chain B, DrmMII or gp28); and the NTases of bacteriophage Bcp1 (YP_009031408) and Erinnyis ello granulovirus (YP_009091875), each with two UTP molecules and three Mg2+ ions (chain A, NTase; chains B and C, UTP; Mg2+ ions are omitted from the PAE matrices). The Acr and Ocr complexes were re-predicted on AlphaFold Server from the same sequences to obtain PAE (one seed, five diffusion samples), and the top-ranked model is shown. For each NTase, five seeds with five diffusion samples each were run (25 models), and a model with the highest ranking score is shown.
